    Model by moving the form

    1. Choose a starting concept and a plan silhouette.
    2. Pull the plan handles to resize its footprint.
    3. Drag the size and twist profiles to change floor plates with height. On a focused point, left/right arrows change the value and up/down arrows move intermediate points through height.
    4. Choose a diagonal concept and inspect the Structure view.
    5. Inspect Terrace connection to see level decks and support beams. Edit Podium plan and open public ground, then use Move whole building to place the complete volume on the site.
    6. Save an option, share its JSON or export a native Rhino model.

    The Babel option combines a tapered spiral with exterior planted terraces. It is your team's interpretation of the references.
